Common Challenges Schools Face

Transportation management shouldn't keep parents and administrators awake at night

No Visibility

Parents have no idea where their child's bus is, causing anxiety and constant calls to school office asking for updates.

Unpredictable Delays

Traffic jams, breakdowns, or route deviations happen with no way to inform parents in advance about delays or changes.

No Boarding Confirmation

Did my child board the bus? Parents have no confirmation that their child actually got on the bus safely.

Manual Attendance

Drivers tracking attendance on paper leads to errors, lost records, and no real-time data for administrators.

Driver Accountability

No way to monitor driver behavior, route adherence, speed limits, or unauthorized stops during the journey.

Communication Gaps

Parents calling school, school calling drivers, drivers unable to respond—communication chaos during emergencies.

Complete School Transport Ecosystem

Our school transport management system helps schools manage their transportation services with integrated modules that work together to optimize routes, track vehicles in real-time, monitor driver behavior, and communicate with parents instantly.

From route planning and vehicle maintenance tracking to student attendance monitoring via RFID cards—everything is automated, real-time, and accessible from mobile apps.

Designed to be user-friendly and easy to use for school administrators, bus drivers, and parents alike.

Core Features

Everything you need for complete transport management

Real-Time GPS Tracking

Track every bus in your fleet live on a map. Know exact location, speed, route adherence, and estimated arrival time for every vehicle.

RFID Attendance

HF/UHF combo cards for students. Tap card when boarding/alighting the bus. Automatic attendance logging with instant parent notifications.

Auto Parent Alerts

SMS and push notifications when child boards bus, alights at home/school, and bus departure/arrival alerts with ETA.

Route Optimization

Intelligent route planning to minimize travel time and fuel costs. Auto-generate optimal pickup and drop sequences.

Driver Behavior Monitoring

Track speed violations, harsh braking, sudden acceleration, and unauthorized stops. Generate driver performance reports.

Vehicle Maintenance

Track service schedules, fuel consumption, spare parts inventory, and maintenance costs. Get alerts before due dates.

Geofencing & Alerts

Set virtual boundaries. Get alerts when buses enter/exit school zones, unauthorized route deviations, or speed limit violations.

Emergency SOS

Panic button in buses for emergencies. Instant alerts to school admin, parents, and authorities with live location.

Analytics & Reports

Comprehensive reports on attendance, route efficiency, fuel consumption, driver performance, and operational costs.

How It Works

Seamless experience for all stakeholders

For Parents

  • Download parent mobile app
  • See live bus location on map
  • Get ETA to pickup/drop point
  • Receive boarding/alighting alerts
  • View attendance history (30 days)
  • Contact driver with one tap
  • Apply for transport leave

For Drivers

  • Start trip from driver mobile app
  • Follow optimized route navigation
  • Students tap RFID cards when boarding
  • Auto-attendance syncs to cloud
  • Parents get instant notifications
  • Complete trip and submit reports
  • Log any incidents or issues

For Administrators

  • Monitor all buses on central dashboard
  • View real-time attendance data
  • Track driver performance metrics
  • Generate compliance reports
  • Manage routes and schedules
  • Handle parent queries efficiently
  • Plan maintenance and costs
