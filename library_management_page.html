How RFID Transforms Libraries

Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology automates and streamlines library processes including check-in, check-out, inventory management, and security.

Each book or media item is tagged with an RFID chip that can be read wirelessly. This enables self-service kiosks, automated returns, faster circulation, and efficient stock-taking.

Our RFID solution integrates seamlessly with Koha, providing a complete automated library system that enhances both staff efficiency and patron experience.
