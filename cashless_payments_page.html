The Problem with Cash-Driven Events

Cash creates chaos at high-footfall events and venues

Slow Transactions

Long queues at counters as staff struggles with cash handling, change giving, and manual billing during peak hours.

Cash Leakage

Revenue leaks through unaccounted cash, missing change, theft, and no way to track what's actually collected.

Reconciliation Nightmare

Hours spent counting cash, matching bills, finding discrepancies. No real-time visibility into collections.

Poor Customer Experience

Frustrated customers wait in long lines, carry exact change, and face transaction errors during events.

No Data Insights

Impossible to know what's selling, peak hours, popular items, or customer behavior patterns.

Security Risks

Cash handling creates theft risk, counterfeit currency issues, and safety concerns for staff and organizers.

How It Works

Simple 3-component ecosystem

1

Smart Cards / QR Codes

Issue prepaid RFID cards or QR codes to customers. They load money once and use for all purchases throughout the event.

2

Android POS App

Vendors use simple Android app on tablets/phones. Tap card or scan QR to complete payment. Works 100% offline—no Wi-Fi or LAN required.

3

Admin Dashboard

Monitor transactions by counter, user, or time with automated reconciliation. Generate reports instantly from web dashboard.
