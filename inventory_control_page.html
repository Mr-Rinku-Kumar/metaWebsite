Common Inventory Challenges

If you operate canteens, hostels, or food services across campuses

No Visibility

Can't see what's in stock across multiple locations. No idea what's expiring soon or what needs urgent restocking.

Food Wastage

Overstock of perishables leads to spoilage. Understock causes menu changes and dissatisfied customers. No data to optimize.

Inaccurate Costing

No link between meals served and actual ingredient consumption. Can't calculate true cost per plate or identify profit leaks.

Manual Records

Dependency on Excel sheets and paper registers. Data errors, missing entries, and impossible to get real-time reports.

Supply Chain Chaos

Untracked material movement between central warehouse and outlets. No approval workflow. Mystery shortages and pilferage.

No Forecasting

Ordering based on guesswork. No historical data to predict requirements. Either overstocking or stock-outs every time.
